settings.companionNote	Open the toolbox at the top left, tap Buddy, pick Yiye, start. The ding means it’s listening: use your phone as usual and just ask out loud. Your words and the screen at that moment go to your own butler, which listens, looks, and answers out loud. The dot on the toolbox: green is listening, blinking means it hears you, orange is the butler on it, red is something broke (it’ll tell you what). Lock the phone with Buddy on, or tap Deep talk in the toolbox, for deep talk: no screen, start with “大管家”, it thinks for a few minutes and reads you the answer; the full text lands in the 深聊 folder. Say “大管家，结束” to stop. The red and orange dots up top are Apple’s broadcast and mic lights. Normal.
